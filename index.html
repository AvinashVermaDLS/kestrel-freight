Kestrel Freight Ltd

Road freight and warehousing, Dublin and Cork, since 2009

About us

Kestrel Freight runs 140 trucks and two warehouses for retailers across Ireland. We employ about 600 people, and we're growing: roughly 40 new drivers and warehouse staff join us every quarter.

People development

Our People Development team is led by Niamh Kavanagh. New starters spend two days in a classroom at our Cork depot before they shadow a senior colleague for their first month. We know two days of slides isn't the best start, and in 2027 we want to move our onboarding to short digital modules that drivers can do on a tablet before their first shift.
